Privacy Policy

How the shortuploader Windows desktop app handles your data.

About Terms of Service Privacy Policy Contact

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: everything stays on your own computer. shortuploader has no server, collects no analytics and shares nothing with the developer or any third party. Data is sent only to TikTok and Google/YouTube, and only to publish the videos you choose.

Data the app uses

DataWhyWhere it is kept
TikTok access token, open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic)To show which TikTok account is signed inOn your computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI
TikTok creator settings (nickname, allowed privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length)Shown on the posting screen before every post, as TikTok requiresIn memory only while the app runs
Video file, title, caption, hashtags, privacy level, interaction and commercial content settings (video.publish, video.upload)Sent to TikTok when you press Publish or Send to draftsYour files stay on your computer
Google sign-in token, YouTube channel name, avatar and playlistsTo upload to your YouTube channel and list your uploadsToken encrypted with DPAPI on your computer
Publishing history (title, date, video IDs) and optional archive copies of published videosTo show your uploaded videos in the appOn your computer only

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and the developer receives no data at all.
  • It has no backend server, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.
  • It does not read your followers, messages, other videos or any TikTok data beyond what is listed above.

TikTok

TikTok's handling of your data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.

Google and YouTube

shortuploader uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you sign out in the app or delete the app's keys folder. Signing out deletes the stored token and revokes it with the service. Uninstalling the app and deleting its folders removes all data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly process their data.
