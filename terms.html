1. What the app does

shortuploader runs on your own Windows computer. You choose video files on your computer, enter a title, description and hashtags, and the app publishes those videos to your TikTok account and/or your YouTube channel using the official TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API. It can also download recordings of your own Twitch broadcasts to your computer.

2. Your accounts

You sign in to TikTok and Google on their official pages in your web browser. The app never sees or stores your passwords. You can sign out in the app at any time and revoke the app's access in your TikTok and Google account settings.

  • Nothing is published until you press Publish in the app. For TikTok you choose who can see each video and confirm TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation (and the Branded Content Policy when you disclose branded content).
  • If your video promotes a brand, product or service, you must disclose it using the commercial content disclosure in the app.
